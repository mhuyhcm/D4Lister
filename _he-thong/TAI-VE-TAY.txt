D4Lister can hai chuong trinh phu
=================================

Binh thuong KHONG phai doc file nay. Bam dup CAI-DAT.bat la no tu kiem tra
may ban co chua, thieu cai nao thi tu tai cai do ve.

File nay danh cho luc may chan mang, hoac ban muon tu cai.


1. AutoHotkey 1.1   (~3 MB)   -- BAT BUOC, khong co thi khong chay duoc
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tai:   https://www.autohotkey.com/download/1.1/AutoHotkey_1.1.37.02_setup.exe
Trang: https://www.autohotkey.com/download/1.1

Chay bo cai, bam  Express Installation.  Cai vao dau cung duoc.

LUU Y: phai la ban 1.1, KHONG phai ban 2. Hai ban viet khac cu phap,
D4Lister.ahk viet bang 1.1.


2. Duong ong TTS   (~110 KB)   -- BAT BUOC cho ban v3
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
V3 KHONG dung Tesseract nua. Thay vao do, game Diablo 4 doc chu item ra
thang cho tool qua mot duong ong.

Binh thuong: thoat han game roi chay  _he-thong\CAI-TTS.cmd  la xong.

Muon lam tay:

   a) Tai file nay:
      https://raw.githubusercontent.com/d4lfteam/d4lf/main/tts/saapi64.dll
      (cua du an D4LF, giay phep MIT)

   b) Ky no bang mot chung chi tu ky - game chi nap DLL CO chu ky.
      Mo PowerShell roi chay:

        $c = New-SelfSignedCertificate -Type CodeSigningCert `
               -Subject 'CN=D4Lister TTS' -CertStoreLocation 'Cert:\CurrentUser\My'
        Set-AuthenticodeSignature -FilePath '<duong dan saapi64.dll>' -Certificate $c

   c) Chep file da ky vao thu muc cai Diablo 4 (canh Diablo IV.exe).

   d) Bat game, vao Options bat ba cong tac:
        Accessibility : Use Screen Reader
                        3rd Party Screen Reader
        Gameplay      : Advanced Tooltip Information

Chung chi nam trong kho CA NHAN cua tai khoan Windows, KHONG vao kho tin cay
he thong. Chi THEM mot file vao thu muc game, khong sua file nao cua game.

Muon huy: xoa saapi64.dll trong thu muc game, va xoa chung chi trong
certmgr.msc > Personal > Certificates.
