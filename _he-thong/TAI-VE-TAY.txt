D4Lister can hai chuong trinh phu
=================================

Binh thuong KHONG phai doc file nay. Bam dup CAI-DAT.bat la no tu kiem tra
may ban co chua, thieu cai nao thi tu tai cai do ve.

File nay danh cho luc may chan mang, hoac ban muon tu cai.


1. AutoHotkey 1.1   (~3 MB)   -- BAT BUOC, khong co thi khong chay duoc
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tai:   https://www.autohotkey.com/download/1.1/AutoHotkey_1.1.37.02_setup.exe
Trang: https://www.autohotkey.com/download/1.1

Chay bo cai, bam  Express Installation.  Cai vao dau cung duoc.

LUU Y: phai la ban 1.1, KHONG phai ban 2. Hai ban viet khac cu phap,
D4Lister.ahk viet bang 1.1.


2. Tesseract   (~55 MB)   -- khong bat buoc
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thieu Tesseract thi tool van chay, chi la khong doc duoc chu trong anh:
ban phai bam SCAN tren trang roi tu sua so bang tay.

Cach de nhat - ban xach tay, khong phai cai:

   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mhuyhcm/D4Lister/acd1f63a41fe4c64c9a0b5d6d829cbe3217e2b0f/_he-thong/bo-cai/tesseract-portable.zip

Tai ve, bung thang vao thu muc D4Lister. Bung xong phai co:

   D4Lister\tesseract\tesseract.exe

Hoac cai ban chinh thuc (trang cua dai hoc Mannheim, ban Windows chinh chu):

   https://github.com/UB-Mannheim/tesseract/wiki

Cai o dau cung duoc, D4Lister tu do o ba cho:

   D4Lister\tesseract\tesseract.exe
   C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR\tesseract.exe
   C:\Program Files (x86)\Tesseract-OCR\tesseract.exe
